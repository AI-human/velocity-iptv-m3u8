Android Setup (Chrome)

Step 1: Copy the code below.

Step 2: Bookmark any random page right now (tap the ⭐ icon in the Chrome menu).

Step 3: Edit that new bookmark. Change its Name to "🔄 Refresh IPTV" and paste the copied code into the URL field, then save it.

How to Run it on Android Chrome (Important!)

1. Open ajobtv.com on your phone browser.

2. Tap the Chrome address bar, and type: Refresh IPTV

⚠️ CRITICAL STEP:
Look at the autocomplete dropdown list. You MUST tap the suggestion that has the star ⭐ icon next to it (it will show javascript:... under it).

Do NOT press "Go" or "Enter" on your keyboard, and do NOT tap the suggestion with the magnifying glass 🔍 icon, otherwise Chrome will just do a Google search instead of running the script.

3. Once you tap the starred suggestion, you will see status toasts appear on the screen verifying the scrape and gist update.
